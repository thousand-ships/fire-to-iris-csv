FIRE to IRIS CSV

Turns a FIRE (IRS Publication 1220) .txt file into IRIS Taxpayer Portal CSV files. Handles 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC for tax year 2025 only. Runs entirely in this page: no uploads and no network (a Content-Security-Policy blocks all connections), so TINs never leave your computer.

Open source (MIT). Same logic as the fire_to_iris.py command-line tool, tested against it on synthetic files. Not yet tested with a real IRIS portal upload. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the IRS.
